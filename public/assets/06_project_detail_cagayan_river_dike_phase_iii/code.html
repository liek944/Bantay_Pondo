Red Flag #1 Severe Negative Slippage (-38.4%)

Exceeds Mandatory Termination Threshold under RA 9184

Pursuant to Annex I (Guidelines on Contract Termination) of the 2016 Revised IRR of RA 9184, negative slippage exceeding 15.0% triggers immediate default proceedings, contract forfeiture, and blacklisting recommendations. While DPWH RO2 approved Time Extension #2 citing unresolved right-of-way (ROW) easement, Tuguegarao City Assessor records confirm the Buntun coastal easement had already been acquired and cleared in August 2022.
