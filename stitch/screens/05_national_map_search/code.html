Independent Civil Society Verification Pipeline

All disbursement metrics are ingested bi-weekly from the Commission on Audit (COA) Annual Audit Reports, PhilGEPS Notice of Award registers, and DPWH Civil Works registry schemas. Geohazard boundaries utilize 100-year return period flood levels calculated by the UP Resilience Institute (Project NOAH) and Mines and Geosciences Bureau (MGB).
