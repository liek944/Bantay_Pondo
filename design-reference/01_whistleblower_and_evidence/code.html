Field Integrity Protocol

To ensure your submission holds weight in official COA audits and newsroom reporting, observe these four verification protocols:

  1. 01.
    Fixed Geographic Landmarks Frame kilometer posts (K-posts), bridge piers, river confluences, or barangay boundary markers alongside the vacant project alignment.
  2. 02.
    Project Billboard & Tarpaulin Photograph the mandated COA/DPWH transparency sign showing the contractor name, project cost, and target completion date.
  3. 03.
    Continuous Video Walkthrough If walking the site, capture an unbroken 60-second video panning 360 degrees from horizon to ground to rule out selective framing.
  4. 04.
    Safety & Zero Trespass Never enter high-velocity river channels or restricted quarry compounds. Drone aerial overflights or public easement vantage points are fully admissible.
